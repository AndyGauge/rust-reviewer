cutile-rs vs candle · NVIDIA GB10 (sm_121) · f16 · 4096 rows

cuTile is 6× faster than the model's op-by-op kernels, and 1.1–2.9× faster than candle's fused ones at wide rows

The same RMSNorm and softmax on the Ascent GX10, three ways. "candle op-by-op" is what reviewer-train runs today. "candle fused" is candle-nn's single-kernel version. At narrow rows (N of 1024 to 2048) candle's fused softmax is the faster one.

Throughput counts one read plus one write of the 4096 × N f16 tensor, the same definition the cutile-rs benchmark uses. The harnesses differ: cuTile numbers are criterion medians, candle numbers are the median of 7 batches of 100 calls with a device sync per batch, cycling through 8 input tensors to avoid re-reading L2. GPU idle, clocks not locked. Fused softmax at N=1024 reads 285 GB/s, above the 273 GB/s spec line (remembered from the datasheet, not checked), so some caching survives at small N and that point is the least reliable.
